About the game

Wing Dodge 3D is a bird-flying arcade game that runs entirely in your web browser — there is nothing to download or install. You flap to stay in the air while obstacles close in from all four sides: the floor, the ceiling, and both walls. Collect stars as you fly, then spend them in the shop on new birds and cosmetics, or on reviving after a crash.

The game is built with Three.js and runs on phones, tablets and desktop computers. It is available in 15 languages, including English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Russian.

Four worlds

  • Forest — the starting world. Weave between branches and bushes.
  • Temple — Greek columns push in from every side through a stone facade.
  • Glass — giant shards of broken window jut into your path.
  • Backrooms — stacked office furniture in an endless yellow corridor.

Each world is unlocked with a score target on the previous world plus stars you have collected.

Five birds, five skills

  • Chick — an eggshell shield that blocks one crash.
  • Sparrow — shrinks so it can slip through tight gaps.
  • Swallow — pulls nearby stars toward it.
  • Owl — slows the world down for a moment.
  • Eagle — blasts away the obstacles directly ahead.

How to play

  • Tap the screen or press Space to flap upward.
  • Arrow buttons, arrow keys, or tilting your phone to move left and right.
  • The ✨ button or Shift to use your bird's skill.

On phones, landscape orientation gives you a wider view. Controls can be changed in Settings.
